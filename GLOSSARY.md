# SoliLoan

SoliLoan is the context for running a direct-credit (Direktkredit) project: the people, contracts, and money in that project, and the emails and documents generated from them.

## Language

### Credit

**Kredit**:
A loan in a direct-credit project.
_Avoid_: Darlehen

**Kreditgeber*in**:
A person or organization that provides a Kredit. Plural: Kreditgeber*innen.
_Avoid_: Darlehensgeber, Kreditgeber

**Kreditkonto**:
A Kreditgeber*in's combined position in the project across their Kredite.
_Avoid_: Konto, Bankkonto

**Kreditvertrag**:
The contract for one Kredit.
_Avoid_: Darlehensvertrag

**Kreditbetrag**:
The principal amount of a Kredit.
_Avoid_: Darlehensbetrag

**Kreditlaufzeit**:
The contractual length of a Kredit, from the day the contract is signed until that contract ends. A Kredit that ends by notice has no fixed Kreditlaufzeit.
_Avoid_: Gesamtlaufzeit, Laufzeit

**Bisherige Kreditlaufzeit**:
How long a Kredit has already run, from the first deposit until repayment, or until today while the Kredit is still running.
_Avoid_: Kreditlaufzeit, Restlaufzeit

**Kündigungsart**:
How a Kredit is agreed to end: on a fixed date, after a fixed duration, or by a notice period.
_Avoid_: Kündigungstyp, Kündigungsmodalität

**Kündigungsfrist**:
The notice period of a Kredit that can be ended by notice.
_Avoid_: Kündigungsdauer

**Fälligkeitsdatum**:
The date a Kredit falls due for repayment.
_Avoid_: Rückzahlungsfrist, Endedatum

**Rückzahlungsdatum**:
The date a Kredit was actually repaid.
_Avoid_: Fälligkeitsdatum

**Ansparvertrag**:
A Kredit whose Kreditbetrag is paid in scheduled rates.
_Avoid_: Sparvertrag

**Anlageart**:
A category of Kredite grouped for compliance with the Bagatellgrenze, identified by its interest rate.
_Avoid_: Anlageform

**Kontomitteilung**:
A yearly document for one Kreditgeber*in listing their Kredite, Zahlungen and interest for one year.
_Avoid_: Kontoauszug, loan statement

**Bagatellgrenze**:
The limit under § 2 VermAnlG (at most 20 units, or €100,000 within twelve months) up to which an Anlageart needs no prospectus.
_Avoid_: VermAnlG-Limit

### Dashboard

**Benachrichtigung**:
A condition in a Direktkredit project that the person running the project should look at. Plural: Benachrichtigungen.
_Avoid_: Benachrichtigungsart

**Benachrichtigungsart**:
How a Kreditgeber*in is contacted: online, email, or post.
_Avoid_: Benachrichtigung

### Template blocks

**Bedingung**:
Whether a template block is shown. An empty Bedingung means the block is always shown.
_Avoid_: Filter

**Regel**:
One criterion inside a Bedingung. The block is shown only when every Regel matches.
_Avoid_: Filter
